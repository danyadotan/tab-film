# TAB Film — voiceover script
Total 2:20 · ~2.3 words/sec · calm, unhurried, one voice

**0:00 Opening (8s)**
Meet TAB. Not another app — a co-worker that lives inside your browser.

**0:08 Morning (10s)**
Monday, eight-forty. Fourteen tabs, eleven messages, two copies of the same doc. Everything asks for attention at once.

**0:18 Intention (12s)**
So we started with one intention. Center the one thing. Hide the noise — filed, not closed. And speak only once.

**0:30 First map (12s)**
The first map had eight pages. Each one was right. Together, they asked you to hold eight things in your head.

**0:42 The cut (14s)**
So we cut. Waking up and setting the desk became one greeting. Handoff and wrap became one close. Eight pages, four rooms.

**0:56 Rule of one (10s)**
And one rule held it together. One voice: a single line. One place: the amber tab. One color, TAB's alone.

**1:06 Day (12s)**
In the morning, TAB is your assistant. Three things, briefed. Then one proposal — pin the spec till noon. You decide.

**1:18 Write (12s)**
While you write, it's an editor. Your words, nothing generated. And just once, uninvited: Northwind in five. I'll hold your place.

**1:30 Call (12s)**
On the call, a wingman. The goal stays pinned. Her likely questions, your answers — ready under the video.

**1:42 Close (12s)**
At the end of the day, a librarian. What got done, two alerts that are real, and a receipt for everything TAB did.

**1:54 For the company (20s)**
Now multiply that by every desk. TAB counts events, not people — anonymous by design. One ledger shows where work stalls, and what happened next. The math is simple: friction events, times the cost of a stall.

**2:14 Outro (6s)**
TAB. One thing at a time.
